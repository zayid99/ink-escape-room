Art direction · round 1

One haunted room, five ways to draw it

Every scene below is the same room running live: the player paces, the candle gutters, and once per loop something stands in the doorway after the player turns away. Hover or drag on a scene to aim the flashlight and catch it. Each style also shows how a clue line would look in its dialogue box.

Letters A–E are for replying in chat.

APixel art

Lantern Pixel

Chunky 16-bit pixels with a 16-colour palette. Light is dithered, so shadows break into texture instead of fading smoothly.

Resolution
160 × 90, scaled up crisp
Palette
16 colours: cold blues, candle ambers, one blood red
Motion
Stepped at 12 fps, sprite frames
Assets
Draw sprites in Aseprite or LibreSprite. Easiest to produce solo.
Performance
Excellent on any device

Fits: retro-creepy feel, fast to build many rooms, clean clue sprites.

Watch out: tiny text on objects must go in close-up views.

B1-bit dither

Two-Tone Dither

Only ink and bone. Everything, including light, is built from ordered dither patterns. Stark and unsettling, like a photocopy of a crime scene.

Resolution
240 × 135
Palette
2 colours
Motion
Stepped at 10 fps
Assets
Flat shapes; the dither is done in code. Cheapest to make.
Performance
Excellent

Fits: a strong, memorable identity on a small budget.

Watch out: busy rooms get noisy; small clues are hard to read without colour.

CPainted

Painted Gloom

Soft painted surfaces under real-time light: candle glow, a moonbeam with dust, drifting fog and film grain. The most cinematic option.

Resolution
Full HD, smooth
Palette
Open, colour-graded toward cold blue
Motion
Smooth 60 fps with eased movement
Assets
A painted background per room. Most art work by far.
Performance
Good; heaviest of the five

Fits: the "finished indie game" look and slow-burn atmosphere.

Watch out: every new room is a painting, so chapter count costs more.

DSilhouette

Backlit Silhouette

Black shapes against pale fog, with rain on the glass and layered mist in front. The doorway glows, so whatever stands in it is pure shadow.

Resolution
Full HD, smooth
Palette
Greyscale plus one warm flame
Motion
Smooth 60 fps, parallax fog
Assets
Outlines only. Medium effort.
Performance
Very good

Fits: dread through shape; scares land hard against bright backgrounds.

Watch out: clues lose detail, so inspect close-ups must be drawn separately.

EInk sketch

Ink Sketchbook

The room is drawn as pages of someone's journal: shaky ink lines that never sit still, hatching for shadow, red pen for notes and fire. The figure appears as a gap in the ink.

Resolution
Full HD
Palette
Ink, paper, red pen
Motion
Line boil at 10 fps, hand-drawn feel
Assets
Line drawings (or procedural lines). Medium effort.
Performance
Very good

Fits: an unreliable narrator. The art itself can lie, get crossed out, or be redrawn.

Watch out: less "scary dark", more "deeply wrong". Tension comes from the story.

Animation

Motion feel

The same walk cycle with three animation treatments. Any of these can pair with most styles above, though some pair more naturally (stepped with A and B, smooth with C and D, boil with E).

1 · 8 fps

Frame-by-frame

Snappy, deliberate poses. Classic sprite feel and the cheapest to animate.

2 · 60 fps

Smooth rig

Bone-style puppet with lean, bob and a trailing coat. Cinematic and fluid.

3 · 12 fps

Line boil

Outlines redraw every frame so the drawing seems alive and slightly unstable.
